This talk covers verifiable credentials and their role in skills interoperability.

A verifiable credential is a tamper-evident claim about a person that can be checked
cryptographically without contacting the issuer, which makes skills portable across employers.

The speaker stressed that credential issuers carry a governance responsibility, because a
poorly scoped credential spreads ambiguous skill claims throughout the ecosystem.

Adoption depends on shared schemas, so standards bodies and platform vendors need to align on
how a skill is named, scoped, and expired.
