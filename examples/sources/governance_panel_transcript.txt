Welcome to the panel on skills governance in modern talent platforms.

The first principle we keep returning to is that a skills framework must be auditable, which
means every inferred skill should trace back to an observable signal such as a project, an
assessment, or a verified credential.

Panelists agreed that governance should not be treated as a one-time configuration, because
the skills landscape shifts as tools, roles, and regulations change over time.

A recurring recommendation was to publish the taxonomy and its change history openly, so that
employees and auditors can review how competency definitions evolve.
